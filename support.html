Support & instructions

AUTO: listen to music

  1. Open BPM Buddy on Apple Watch.
  2. Allow microphone access when prompted.
  3. Play audible music nearby, keeping the microphone uncovered.
  4. Wait a few seconds for a tempo estimate. Tap AUTO to pause or resume.

TAP: set the tempo yourself

Tap the circle or TAP repeatedly in time with the music. This stops the microphone and switches to manual tap tempo. AUTO returns to listening. Gentle click haptics acknowledge your taps.

The ring and decimal

One full revolution represents four beats at the displayed tempo. Swipe left or right over the circle to change between color and monochrome. The app remembers your theme. A decimal indicates display resolution, not guaranteed accuracy. Musical half/double interpretations are possible.

When the screen dims

Listening continues for about five seconds before pausing. Wake within that window to keep the same measurement. After a longer pause, AUTO resumes when the app returns to the foreground. A manual pause or TAP stays paused. The ring itself pauses in the dimmed display state.

No useful reading?

Try music with a clearer pulse, move closer or reduce nearby noise. Avoid covering the microphone. If the tempo feels wrong, use TAP. This app measures music tempo, not heart rate.

Microphone permission

If access was denied, enable BPM Buddy's microphone permission in the Watch's Settings under Privacy & Security. TAP and the generated demo can be used without microphone access.

Try without music

Tap the small info button at the upper left, then “Try 120 BPM demo”. A generated rhythm runs through the real detector. The display says DEMO, and the result may be close to rather than exactly 120.0.
