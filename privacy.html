Privacy notice

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Your music stays on your Apple Watch.

Microphone audio

BPM Buddy uses the microphone to estimate musical tempo. Audio is processed ephemerally in memory on the Watch. It is not written to an audio file, sent to a server, shared with the developer, or used to train a model. The model is bundled with the app and runs locally.

No automatic data collection

The app does not collect personal data. There is no account, analytics, advertising, tracking, location access or HealthKit integration. No internet connection is required for tempo detection.

Local preferences

The chosen ring theme is saved in the app's own local preferences. This setting is not sent to us. Removing the app removes its local app data, subject to Apple's device and backup handling.

You control listening

Apple controls microphone permission and the system microphone indicator. Tap AUTO to stop listening immediately. TAP also stops the microphone. Capture continues for approximately five seconds after the display dims and then pauses; normal foreground AUTO use can resume on return.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, cookies, advertising or third-party embeds. GitHub may process technical connection information to operate its hosting service; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Support and external websites

External links open sites with their own privacy practices. Those sites and their hosting providers may process connection information separately from this offline app. If you voluntarily email support, the information you send is used to respond to your request. This is separate from automatic collection by the app.

Changes

This notice will be updated if the app's data practices change.
