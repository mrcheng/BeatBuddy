BPM Buddy · Wear OS

Microphone service demonstration

AUTO estimates music tempo locally and preserves a measurement through a brief interruption.

What the video shows

  1. The built-in generated 120 BPM demo runs through the bundled tempo detector. It is identified as DEMO.
  2. TAP stops the demo. AUTO starts microphone capture, indicated by the app and Android’s microphone status.
  3. Returning after a brief background period preserves the current listening session.
  4. Leaving for longer than five seconds stops microphone capture and removes foreground-service status.
  5. Returning resumes AUTO. An explicit AUTO pause stops capture immediately.

Review environment

The emulator’s host microphone input is disabled. AUTO therefore receives silence; it does not demonstrate real-music accuracy. The opening demo uses generated audio locally. This video is a silent screen recording and contains no microphone recording.

Why the service is needed

Tempo estimation needs a continuous, immediate audio stream. A fixed five-second grace period lets the user briefly lower or leave the watch display without restarting the measurement. Capture does not continue indefinitely in the background. The microphone foreground notification includes a Pause action; AUTO and TAP also stop capture. Audio stays in bounded memory and is never saved, uploaded or used for training.
